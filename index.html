About Hi Girl

A clinic built on trust, honest advice & visible results

Hi Girl Advanced Cosmetology Center sits on Yendada Main Road in Visakhapatnam, dedicated to skin, hair and laser care in a calm, hygienic, hospital-clean space. Every consultation starts with an honest skin or scalp analysis — no upselling, no guesswork — and every treatment room is equipped with advanced, well-maintained technology.

From HydraFacials and laser hair reduction to PRP hair therapy and body contouring, our promise is simple: trust, transparency and transformation in every visit.

  • Hygienic, doctor-supervised treatment rooms
  • Transparent pricing & consultation before every procedure
  • Skin, hair & laser specialists under one roof

Our Team

Dr. Suresh Naik

Clinical Dermatologist & Aesthetic Physician

Dermatology · Trichology · Cosmetology · Nutrition

Dr. Suresh Naik is a Clinical Dermatologist and Aesthetic Physician with over 15 years of experience, specialising in holistic skin rejuvenation, advanced hair restoration and medical-grade cosmetic procedures. His approach combines rigorous medical protocols with a personalised touch, ensuring every client achieves natural, long-lasting results.
